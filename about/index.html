About ChillSpace

Feel like social, no need for network.

What is ChillSpace?

ChillSpace is a placebo social media network — a simulated social platform that gives you the rituals of posting, liking, and commenting without the addictive dopamine loops of mainstream sites. It is a website and app that gives you the feeling of scrolling social media, but without social media, and it is designed to make you happy.

The tagline is "Feel like social, no need for network" — because the point is the feeling of connection, not the network effect that monetises it.

Why it exists

Most social media is designed to make you angry and sad, so you stay stuck chasing the little bit of happiness it occasionally gives you. That mechanism is called variable reward: sometimes you see something that makes you happy, sometimes something that makes you angry, and the unpredictability is what keeps you scrolling. It is the same psychology as a slot machine — every scroll is a spin.

ChillSpace replaces variable reward with consistent reward. Every post is designed to leave you feeling good, so you can feel satisfied without being hooked. The goal is simple: help you find happiness, so you do not have to keep searching.

What makes it different

  • No algorithmic timeline. Posts appear in random order, not optimised to keep you engaged.
  • No push notifications. No pinging, no nagging, no anxiety loop.
  • No engagement optimisation. No rage-bait, no engagement farming — even the debate posts are light-hearted.
  • No data collection. ChillSpace does not collect any data. Not even a hit counter. Cloudflare collects essential data to host the site, but ChillSpace itself collects absolutely nothing. The Android app works without internet, so not even Cloudflare gets data when you use the app.
  • No ads. ChillSpace does not contain ads and does not use Google Analytics or any other advertising and tracking technologies.
  • No account required. You do not have to create an account or log in — you do not even have to close a cookie pop-up.

What you actually do there

You see a feed of fun, random posts designed to make you happy. You can post, and you will get guaranteed likes — placebo likes that make you feel good without being from real people. There is a Daily Challenge, which is something little to be proud of yourself for, and another reason to be happy. The Android app lets you scroll the ChillSpace placebo feed and the ReelSpace video feed without any internet — even on a plane.

The ChillSpace family

ChillSpace is one of several placebo tools built around the same principle of satisfying simulation without real-world cost.

  • ReelSpace — a mindful short-form video alternative to TikTok, Shorts, and Reels. It encourages intentional viewing over compulsive scrolling.
  • ChillSpace Shop — a simulator that mimics the SHEIN shopping experience: browse, add to cart, checkout, without any real purchases or financial consequences.
  • BuyVinyl — a simulator for ordering vinyl records. Once you "receive" your record, the site plays real music with a spinning record animation.
  • Number Game — a pure, no-ads, no-microtransaction clicker game that gives you the satisfaction of progress without the addiction loop.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChillSpace a real social network?
It is a placebo social media network. You can post, reply, like, and message, but the goal is not to trap you in an engagement loop. It is a calm, simulated social experience designed to satisfy rather than addict.
Does ChillSpace collect my data?
No. ChillSpace does not collect any data. Not even a hit counter. Cloudflare collects essential data to host the site, but ChillSpace collects absolutely nothing. The app works without internet, so no data leaves your device when you use it.
What is the difference between ChillSpace and other social media?
ChillSpace removes the variable reward mechanism. Instead of showing you content designed to provoke anger, fear, or envy so you keep scrolling, every post is designed to be pleasant. The result is a feed you can leave satisfied, not drained.
Do I need an account?
No. You do not have to create an account or log in, and you do not even have to close a cookie pop-up.
Is there an app?
Yes. The Android app is available on Google Play, the Amazon Appstore, GitHub Releases, and APKPure. Once installed, it lets you scroll the ChillSpace placebo feed and ReelSpace video feed without any internet — even on a plane.
Is ChillSpace open source?
Yes, under the AGPL-3.0 licence. The web version is on GitHub at github.com/op-tube/chillspace, and the Android app is at github.com/op-tube/ChillSpaceApp.
Which ChillSpace is the real one?
