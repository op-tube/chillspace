Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about ChillSpace.

What is ChillSpace? +
ChillSpace is a placebo social media network – a simulated social platform that offers the rituals of interaction (posting, liking, commenting) without the addictive dopamine loops found on mainstream sites.
Why does ChillSpace exist? +
It was created to provide a space for people who enjoy the social aspects of sharing and connecting, but want to avoid the psychological manipulation, algorithmic feeds, and endless engagement tactics typical of commercial social media.
How is ChillSpace different from other social media? +
ChillSpace has no algorithmic timeline, no infinite scroll, no push notifications, and no engagement‑optimization. Posts appear in random order (no addictive algorithms!), interactions are low‑pressure, and the entire experience is intentionally ‘dumbed down’ to mimic a social feed without the addictive feedback.
Is ChillSpace a real social network? +
No – it functions as a website where you can create posts, follow ‘others’, and interact, but its design philosophy is to be a ‘placebo’ version, so you get the feel of social media without the harmful psychological effects.
Is ChillSpace a ‘dopamine site’? +
Yes, ChillSpace is related to the viral South Korean concept of ‘dopamine sites’ - however our site is more like serotonin - the chemical that makes us feel satisfied and excited. So, while our site can be considered a ‘dopamine site’, it is more accurately a ‘serotonin site’ or ‘placebo social media’
