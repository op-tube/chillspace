Privacy Policy

ChillSpace · Last updated: August 2026

ChillSpace (the “Platform”) is built with your privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices across the entire site – including the main feed, posts, comments, messages, daily challenges, and AI features.

1. Information we collect

We collect very little personal information, and we never store it on our servers.

  • Local data: All user‑generated content – posts, comments, likes, your display name, messages, contacts, daily challenge progress – is stored only on your device using your browser’s localStorage. We do not have access to this data.
  • No account creation: We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any other identifier from you.

When you create or share content (posts, comments, messages), we generate a ChillSpace link that may include the following temporary, query‑string parameters:

  • Your chosen display name (post_creator or sender_name)
  • Your content (post_content or message_content)
  • Optional image/video links (img_src, video_src)
  • Timestamps or comment threads (comment_creator, reply1_creator, etc.)

These parameters exist in the URL you generate to share content. Please note that when sharing links on third-party platforms, those services may process or log the URL to generate link previews.

2. How we use your information

  • To display your content: The feed shows posts and comments that you and others create – all locally generated or shared via links.
  • To enable AI features: “ChillSpace AI” analyses post text using browser‑side logic and speaks responses using the Web Speech API (or the native Android TTS bridge in the app). No data is sent to an external AI service, except when you send a message to ChatGPT via ChillSpace Messages, which opens the ChatGPT website.
  • To track daily challenges: Your progress (scrolls, messages, song rankings, number game completions) is stored locally to help you complete optional goals.
  • To improve your experience: We may use local data to remember your preferences (e.g., your display name).

3. How messages and posts are shared

When you share a post or send a message, the Platform:

  • Builds a ChillSpace link that contains your content and identity.
  • Opens the relevant sharing method (e.g., WhatsApp, email, Instagram, ChatGPT, or a simple copy‑to‑clipboard) so you can manually send it to your chosen recipient.

We do not have access to your messages or posts, nor do we store them on any server. The content is only present in the URL you share.

4. Third‑party services

The Platform integrates with the following external platforms to deliver shared content:

  • WhatsApp – when you share via WhatsApp, you are subject to WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Email – messages are sent via your default email client; we do not collect or store your email address.
  • Instagram – when you share via Instagram Direct, you are subject to Instagram’s privacy policy.
  • ChatGPT – when you send a message to an AI assistant, we open ChatGPT’s site with a pre‑filled prompt; you are subject to OpenAI’s privacy policy.
  • Twitter (X) – when you share via Twitter, you are subject to Twitter’s privacy policy.

We do not control these third‑party services and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

💡 Your data stays on your device. We never see your posts, comments, or messages – they exist only in your browser and the URLs you choose to share.

5. AI and speech synthesis

The “ChillSpace AI” feature analyses post text entirely in your browser to generate helpful, sometimes humorous, responses. It does not send any data to a server.

  • Text‑to‑speech: When you play a story or listen to an AI response, the audio is generated by your browser’s built‑in Web Speech API (or the native TTS engine in the Android app). No audio data is transmitted externally.
  • No AI training: We do not use your posts or comments to train any machine learning models.

6. Data storage and security

All data (posts, comments, display name, contacts, messages, daily challenge progress) is stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

  • localStorage is accessible only by the same origin (chill-space.pages.dev) and cannot be read by other websites.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • We do not host a central database or user accounts, meaning we never store your user-generated content on our servers.
  • Essential network traffic data (such as IP addresses) is processed standardly by our web hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) strictly to load the website.

7. Your choices and control

You can clear all stored data at any time by:

  • Using your browser’s “Clear Site Data” or “Clear Storage” option.
  • Manually deleting the following keys from localStorage:
    • cs_username – your display name
    • cs_my_channel and cs_my_handle – your preferred contact info
    • cs_contacts – your saved contacts
    • cs_messages – your message history
    • chillspace_daily_challenge – your daily challenge progress
    • chillspace_personalities – your saved personality profiles

You may also choose not to use specific features – in which case no related data is stored.

8. Children’s privacy

The Platform is not directed at children under 13. Because all user content remains on the local device, parents or guardians can remove all stored information instantly by clearing site data in the browser settings.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
