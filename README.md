# ChillSpace
ChillSpace is a *placebo social media platform*, which means that the posts aren't from real people looking for an argument, but from a JavaScript script putting together random topics and social media-style questions and statements,   
combined with some random posts meant to help you quit social media. It works without internet on the Android app, or if you download the HTML. It's about as "dynamic" a static page can be.

## Social media
Most people just scroll social media because they are looking for something, maybe something to calm them down, or something to make them happy. Social media companies know this.   
And they know what kind of people don't need that. **the people who are already calm and happy.** So they obviously don't want you to feel satisfied after your scroll, because then you won't need to *keep* scrolling.
So they show you bad news, pointless drama and hateful comments so you feel you need to keep coming back. Then they show you the occasional fun post so you *think* you actually like social media. That's called *variable reward*.   
We have thought of a simple solution: something you mindlessly scroll, that is *actually* designed to make you happy. At first, my idea was to build a *good social network*, but seeing the rise of dopamine sites in Asia, I realised: maybe we *don't need* the network.

## Introduction to ChillSpace
ChillSpace posts might look a bit weird. But we assure you, there are weirder social medias. Under every post, you will see a like button with a random number, a comment button, a repost button and a "ChillSpace AI" button.   
The ChillSpace AI button seldom works, and when it does it gives a pointless explanation of the post. Similar to the Gemini button on YouTube. Except ChillSpace AI is not really an LLM, it doesn't use any water, and it's written in client-side JavaScript. Just like the rest of ChillSpace.

### Why are there games on ChillSpace?
ChillSpace gaming exists for a reason: it takes the variable reward out of gaming. You can't lose in the Number Game or the K-pop game. The Surfer Game is so buggy, I decided to leave it that way. The Jump game is easy.   

### ReelSpace
ReelSpace is a short video simulator. Watch "people" play games with TTS stories over the top and music that was generated by ChillSpace Music.

### ChillSpace Music
A music generation tool. It uses XML, and can be played directly on the web. It is still in beta.

### ChillSpace Search
ChillSpace Search is a search engine that runs client-side. I am considering adding it to the ChillSpace app, so you can search the web offline! Would it be the first offline web search engine? I may also add an instant answer engine that answers thousands or millions of questions.   
#### Why ChillSpace Search is needed
1. Regular search engines actually don't want you to find the answer. They want you to keep scrolling so they can track you and serve you ads.
2. DuckDuckGo is hosted on AWS. Even if DuckDuckGo don't want your data, Amazon will. But with ChillSpace, all your searches run client-side. So no one can access your data, not even your ISP.
3. Vibes. What normal search engine is so colourful?

### ChillSpace Shop
ChillSpace Shop is a dopamine shop. It is different from other dopamine shops in that we don't collect any data and sell it to real shops so they can sell you stuff for real.

### ChillSpace Messages
ChillSpace Messages is a service where you can send messages to bots for fun. No token-burning, it's all JavaScript. On the web version, you can also send messages to humans via another messaging app. On the app version, you can't. It's a pure dopamine service. The web version will soon reflect that.
