Privacy Policy

ChillSpace Messages · Last updated: August 2026

ChillSpace Messages (the “App”) is designed with your privacy in mind. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

We collect very little personal information, and we never store it on our servers.

  • Local data: All data—including your username, contact list, and message history—is stored only on your device using your browser’s localStorage. We do not have access to this data.
  • No account creation: We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any other identifier from you.

When you send a message, we create a link that includes the following temporary, query‑string parameters:

  • Your chosen username (sender_name)
  • Your preferred contact channel (sender_channel and sender_handle)
  • The message content (message_content)
  • A timestamp (sent_at)

These parameters are only used to pre‑fill the recipient’s setup screen when they open the link. They are not stored on any server—they exist only in the URL that you share.

2. How we use your information

  • To enable messaging: We help you compose and send messages via third‑party services (WhatsApp, email, Instagram, or ChatGPT). The App itself does not send, store, or relay your messages.
  • To improve your experience: We may track your progress in optional daily challenges (e.g., number of messages sent). This progress is stored locally and is not sent to us.

3. How messages are delivered

When you click “Send”, the App:

  • Builds a ChillSpace link that contains your message and identity.
  • Opens the relevant third‑party service (e.g., WhatsApp, your email client, Instagram, or ChatGPT) with that link pre‑filled, so you can manually send it.

We do not have access to your messages, nor do we store them on any server. The message content is only present in the URL you share with your recipient.

4. Third‑party services

The App integrates with the following external platforms to deliver your messages:

  • WhatsApp – when you send a message via WhatsApp, you are subject to WhatsApp’s privacy policy.
  • Email – messages are sent via your default email client; we do not collect or store your email address.
  • Instagram – messages are sent via Instagram’s direct messaging system; we do not collect your Instagram credentials.
  • ChatGPT – when you choose to send a message to an AI assistant, we open ChatGPT’s site with a pre‑filled prompt; you are subject to OpenAI’s privacy policy.

We do not control these third‑party services and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

💡 Your data stays on your device. Messages are sent through other services, and we never see your conversations.

5. Data storage and security

All data (contacts, messages, your username, channel settings) is stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

  • localStorage is accessible only by the same origin (chill-space.pages.dev) and cannot be read by other websites.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • No data is transmitted to our servers, so there is no risk of server‑side breaches.

6. Your choices and control

You can clear all stored data at any time by:

  • Using your browser’s “Clear Site Data” or “Clear Storage” option.
  • Manually deleting the following keys from localStorage: cs_username, cs_my_channel, cs_my_handle, cs_contacts, cs_messages, and chillspace_daily_challenge.

You may also choose not to use the App’s features, in which case no data is stored.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can help them delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
